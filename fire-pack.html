One Burning Heat Seeker

Find the heat. Follow the evidence.

The Fire Pack is the engagement. One burning question, one defined area of examination, and the evidence that can actually answer it. We identify what is known, what is documented, what is missing, inconsistent, unclear, or unsupported—and show you what the record actually says.

01

Define

Name the heat.

We start with the specific concern. Not “Are we compliant?” but the question precise enough to investigate.

02

Trace

Follow the record.

We examine the governing requirement, the relevant records, the actual statement or practice, and the evidence connecting them.

03

Test

Separate fact from assumption.

If the concern is substantiated, we show why. If information is missing, we identify the gap. If the evidence does not support the concern, that is the finding.

04

Return

Make the next move visible.

You receive a bounded finding, an evidence trail, the gaps that remain, and a practical indication of what needs to happen next.
