What Schedule III Won’t Change

A new schedule answers one legal question. The operation still has others.

Live · October 2026
The line

A new schedule answers one legal question. The operation still has others.

← Back to the floor

Regulatory review: October 18, 2026. Sources are linked beside the relevant claims. Unverified later procedural outcomes are identified explicitly.

01 — STATE PERMISSION

The April federal rule does not replace a state’s licensing, testing, packaging, or sales rules. State authorization and federal authorization must each cover the activity.

02 — BANKING

The 2026 GAO banking report documents continuing access challenges and Bank Secrecy Act duties. Schedule III does not require a bank to take a customer. The SAFE Banking proposal should not be described as law.

03 — PRODUCT AND MOVEMENT

Do not assume rescheduling authorizes interstate shipments, supplies FDA approval, or removes federal registration and handling rules. Check the product and transaction against the actual federal rule and the destination’s requirements. A change in classification is not a universal shipping permit.

04 — EMPLOYMENT

Do not assume Schedule III removes workplace testing or changes every employer policy. Identify the applicable employment law, testing program, safety duties, and accommodation rules before changing a procedure. This article establishes no uniform national employment rule.
