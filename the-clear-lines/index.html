What this is

Clarity without flattening.

The Clear Lines is not a glossary, and it does not assume the reader already works in cannabis. It is a public explanation layer for operators, workers, consumers, policymakers, neighbors, and anyone trying to understand the industry without being talked down to.

RegMatrix holds deeper state-specific authority and history. The Clear Lines opens the front door: it makes the language, structures, tensions, and consequences understandable before asking anyone to go deeper.
