What This Is

Signal, Not Noise

Clear-FrontLines is not a blog. It is an article index for dispatches generated from regulatory change detection. Each item is tied to a concrete source: statute, rulemaking, agency memo, enforcement action, or METRC data anomaly.

The feed is strongest at the intersection of real-time signal and historical context — the RegMatrix layer gives the state background; The Clear Lines gives the day-to-day delta.
