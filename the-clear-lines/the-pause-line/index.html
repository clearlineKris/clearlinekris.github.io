The line

A paused proceeding is a process state, not a final legal outcome.

← Back to the floor

Regulatory review: October 18, 2026. Sources are linked beside the relevant claims. Unverified later procedural outcomes are identified explicitly.

01 — WHAT WAS PAUSED?

The October 8 legal report concerns the broader marijuana rescheduling proceeding. It reports a September 29 stay to consider a GAO scheduling report and an October 13 agency-response deadline. No later outcome was verified in this review. The effective April medical rule must be tracked separately.

02 — WHAT COMES NEXT?

The DEA hearing procedures govern hearings, evidence, and subsequent administrative steps. A recommendation, exceptions, agency review, and potential judicial review are distinct events. Follow the DEA proceeding materials for the next actual document; do not assign an effective date from a press prediction.

03 — WHAT SHOULD AN OPERATOR RECORD?

Record the order, its scope, the next deadline, and the rule that remains effective while the process continues. A missed expected date does not prove approval, repeal, or automatic resumption. Keep the status dated and link to the evidence.
