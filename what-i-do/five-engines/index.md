# Five Engines

The operational core of Veridion's product stack — five compliance engines built from years of on-the-ground cannabis operations.

## Engines

| Engine | Domain | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **KiloKeep** | Inventory | Inventory hygiene at scale — METTC records clean, reconciled, audit-ready |
| **PRFCTpack** | Product Integrity | COA inspection engine — validates certificate of analysis against packaging and labeling requirements |
| **RecallRepel** | Risk Intelligence | Predictive recall engine — historical patterns + live METTC data to surface risk before enforcement |
| **RegulaGreen** | Waste Management | End-to-end waste compliance with step-by-step standalone app for destruction procedures |
| **GuaranGreen** | Vendor Network | Peer-to-peer vendor rating system for vetted, compliant supply chain partners |

## Status

- KiloKeep | PRFCTpack | RecallRepel | RegulaGreen — **Active**
- GuaranGreen — **In Evaluation**
