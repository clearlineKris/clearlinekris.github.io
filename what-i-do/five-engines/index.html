RegMatrix Integration

The Five Engines don't operate in isolation. Every engine reads from and writes to the RegMatrix intelligence layer — creating a compounding knowledge flywheel where each operational cycle strengthens the whole system.

KiloKeep → RegMatrix

Inventory variance patterns feed enforcement posture models; reconciliation gaps update control effectiveness scores in CL-CANREG-CTRL-INV-*.

PRFCTpack → RegMatrix

COA failure rates by lab, cannabinoid, and contaminant type populate testing integrity benchmarks in CL-CANREG-AUD-QA-* audit frameworks.

RecallRepel → RegMatrix

Recall precursors and CAPA outcomes enrich the risk intelligence library — new patterns automatically generate CL-CANREG-SOP-RCL-* updates.

RegulaGreen → RegMatrix

Destruction method compliance rates by state feed waste management playbooks; witness gaps trigger CL-CANREG-CHKL-WST-* checklist updates.

GuaranGreen → RegMatrix

Vendor compliance ratings cross-reference enforcement databases; supply chain risk scores feed CL-CANREG-CTRL-VND-* vendor management controls.

Cross-Engine Intelligence

The Triage Matrix (TVKH-TRIAGE-001) routes signals across engines — a RecallRepel signal triggers KiloKeep reconciliation and PRFCTpack COA review automatically.

Engine Philosophy

Five principles that govern how we build, deploy, and evolve every engine.

Operator-First Design

Built for the person working a closing shift — not the compliance officer in a corner office. Every workflow reduces cognitive load, not adds to it.

RegMatrix-Grounded

No engine operates on assumptions. Every rule, threshold, and workflow traces back to sourced law, enforcement history, and operator reality in the RegMatrix backbone.

Audit-Ready by Default

Documentation isn't an afterthought — it's the output. Every engine produces the records an inspector expects to see, formatted to the standard they require.

Interoperable, Not Monolithic

Each engine stands alone but connects via open protocols. Operators adopt what they need; the system compounds value when engines run together.

Continuous Calibration

Engines evolve with the regulatory landscape. RegMatrix signal feeds trigger automatic threshold recalibration and playbook updates — no manual version bumps.

Human in the Loop

AI assists, operators decide. Critical actions — holds, recalls, destruction authorizations — always require human attestation. Guardrails, not autopilot.
