Real-time signal layer for regulatory drift. The home of the article index — enforcement anomalies, METRC contradictions, and policy shifts as they happen.

- Monitors METRC data contradictions and labeling discrepancies
- Flags policy shifts before they ripple into compliance requirements
- Provides an article index — searchable, tagged, cross-referenced
