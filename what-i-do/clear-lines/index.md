# The Clear Lines

A public-facing regulatory signal desk for cannabis operators.

## What It Does

- Tracks meaningful regulatory change
- Flags enforcement drift and operational contradictions
- Explains why a change matters in plain language
- Routes deeper questions into RegMatrix and other ClearLine work

## The Point

The Clear Lines is not here to create more noise. It is here to make movement easier to see and easier to use.

> **Catch the movement. Trace the reason. Route the consequence.**
