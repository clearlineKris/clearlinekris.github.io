- **PRFCTpack** — COA inspection engine. Validates certificate of analysis against packaging and labeling requirements.
- **RecallRepel** — Predictive recall engine. Historical patterns + live METRC data to surface risk signals before enforcement.
- **RegulaGreen** — End-to-end waste management compliance with a step-by-step standalone app.
- **GuaranGreen** — Peer-to-peer vendor rating system for vetted, compliant supply chain partners.
