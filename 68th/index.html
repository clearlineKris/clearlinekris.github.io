What 68th is

The part of the system that faces the human being.

68th Joint Insights is not a second company bolted onto 68th Lat & Lit. It is the relational practice inside it—the place for conversation, independent review and grounded judgment.

The methods can map the rule, structure the record and build the process. Joint Insights asks the next question: What does this mean for the person who has to act?

01

Listen

Begin behind the question.

A record rarely arrives alone. 68th makes room for the concern, operating pressure and practical history that caused someone to bring it forward.

02

Read

Let the evidence speak.

The work is reviewed without presuming either failure or perfection. Confirmation is useful. So is a narrow, honestly named gap.

03

Return

Give clarity back in usable form.

The point is not a dramatic finding. It is a shared understanding of what is true, what matters and what the next move should be.

Why 68th Lat & Lit

Built on the floor. Named for the record.

I’ve been in cannabis since March 10, 2017. Inventory, retail, security, documentation and compliance. I’ve seen how rules behave during a real shift, under real pressure, with real people holding the clipboard.

68th Lat & Lit is what I built from that. Lat is Latin, for the language rules are written in. It’s latitude, for the room you actually have to operate in. And it’s lateral, for looking at a problem from the side instead of head-on. Lit is literature, because the record is a text and somebody’s going to read it. It’s also lit, because this industry is supposed to be fun, and the work should still be done right.

So no hype, and no theory for theory’s sake. Just rules, records and real workflows, with me in the loop. If the record can prove it, I’ll show you how. If it can’t, I’ll tell you that too.

And the 68th? Do the math sometime. You’ll get there.
