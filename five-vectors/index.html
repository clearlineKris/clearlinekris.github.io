The distinction

Not one lens. Not one answer.

The Five Vectors give ClearLine five distinct directions from which to examine an operational question. Each reveals something the others may not.

The methodology behind those directions is proprietary. What matters publicly is the result: a fuller field of view, clearer decisions, and work grounded in evidence.

The ClearLine standard

What the Vectors protect.

01

Evidence

Claims should have something behind them. Context matters as much as the headline.

02

Context

A rule, record, relationship, or event means more when its surrounding conditions remain visible.

03

Distinction

Different problems should not be flattened into the same answer simply because they are convenient to group.

04

Action

Insight has to survive contact with the real operation. The point is usable work.

05

Accountability

People remain responsible for decisions. ClearLine helps make the basis for those decisions clearer.

06

Clarity

The goal is not more information. It is a clearer line through the information that matters.
