Texas

Compassionate Use Program only. Low-THC, limited conditions, slow expansion. Adult-use remains politically distant.

Signal

The Core Problem

Texas authorizes a narrow low-THC medical program under the Compassionate Use Act. License counts are small, product scope is constrained, and the political ceiling on broader legalization is high. Compliance work here is about program boundaries, not open-market scale.

Texas compliance is less about volume and more about staying inside a deliberately narrow box.
Watch

Three Tensions

  • Program scope — Low-THC / high-CBD framing and qualifying conditions define what can be produced and sold.
  • License scarcity — Few licensees; each permit is high-stakes under DPS and health oversight.
  • Legislative lag — Neighboring adult-use markets do not automatically move Austin.
Frame

Statute

Texas Compassionate Use Act and DPS / health department implementing rules. Expansion happens only through the legislature.
