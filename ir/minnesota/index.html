Minnesota

Day-one adult-use market. New agency. Inherited hemp mess. Rules still being written.

Signal

The Core Problem

HF 3595 (2022) opened a de facto recreational hemp-THC market with no tracking and no real enforcement. The Office of Cannabis Management arrived later — and inherited the mess. Operators are building against draft rules that can still shift.

Operators were selling before the agency had a phone number.

Three Tensions

  • Hemp dual-track — Different testing, labels, and tax until the regimes are harmonized.
  • Municipal opt-out — Statewide legalization; local bans and moratoriums decide the real map.
  • Seed-to-sale unselected — Platform choice sets every licensee's daily workflow and the OCM's enforcement data.
