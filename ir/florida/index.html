Florida

Medical-only vertical market. Limited MMTC licenses. Adult-use ballot pressure keeps rising against a tightly controlled DOH regime.

Signal

The Core Problem

Florida runs a closed, vertically integrated medical system. A small number of Medical Marijuana Treatment Centers control cultivation through retail. Compliance is license-scarce and regulator-heavy — and every adult-use push collides with that structure.

In Florida, the license is the market. Everything else is positioning for what comes next.
Watch

Three Tensions

  • Vertical integration — MMTCs own the chain end-to-end; independent retail does not exist under current law.
  • Adult-use trajectory — Ballot and legislative attempts keep the conversion question live for incumbents.
  • DOH enforcement — Office of Medical Marijuana Use sets the inspection and seed-to-sale posture.
Frame

Statute

Amendment 2 (2016) and implementing statutes under the Department of Health / OMMU. Vertical MMTC model remains the core constraint.
