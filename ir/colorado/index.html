The market that wrote the playbook. A decade past Amendment 64, METRC fluency is the price of keeping your license.

The Core Problem

MED enforcement is penalty-driven and data-targeted. Inventory gaps, waste logs, and transport manifests sink licenses — not dramatic diversion schemes. First-offense discrepancies can trigger summary suspension.

The operators who survive inspections aren't the ones with the best lawyers. They're the ones whose trimmers know how to use METRC.

Three Tensions

  • METRC reconciliation — Most common citation. Real-time entry, not end-of-day batching.
  • Local overlay — State rules meet municipal setbacks, odor, and consumption rules that vary by city.
  • ALJ precedent — Adjudication record functionally amends the code — and isn't in most training materials.
