Why ClearLine reads differently

Built inside the operation—not outside the consequences.

I have worked in regulated cannabis operations since 2017, across inventory, retail, security, documentation, and compliance systems. ClearLine comes from seeing how rules behave during a real shift, under real pressure, when a missing explanation can become somebody else's liability.

That is why the work begins with evidence and restraint. The goal is not to make your operation sound broken. The goal is to know what is true—and make the record strong enough to carry it.
