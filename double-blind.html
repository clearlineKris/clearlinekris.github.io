The Fresh Freeze

Fresh eyes. Two locked views. One honest read.

The Double Blind View is the method inside the package. It keeps your selection and mine independent, then applies the same standard to both. The Fresh Freeze is the engagement: the conversation, the locked evidence, the review, and the usable finding you receive.

01

Orient

Name the confidence question.

In a free 60-minute conversation, we identify the process you believe is working, the proof that should carry it, and the question the Freeze needs to answer.

02

Lock

Make two independent selections.

After a brief applicability check, you and ClearLine each choose one exact Evidence Bank item. Both choices are recorded and locked within 24 hours of the opening conversation, then kept unrevealed until both are final.

03

Review

Apply one standard twice.

Each eligible pre-meeting specimen receives the same Rule-to-Record review. The analysis tests the governing requirement, the performed work, and the proof left behind—without allowing either selection to change after the fact.

04

Findings

See where the operation keeps its cool.

You receive a bounded comparison showing what stands on its own, what depends on missing context, what needs repair, and whether any further work is justified.

The Evidence Bank

Five doors into the same operation.

Both selections must come from the universal Bank. The category determines what may enter the Freeze; it does not predetermine whether the evidence is sufficient.

01

Rule

What governs the work: the controlling requirement, approved standard, or documented instruction.

02

Routine

What normally happens, how the process is performed, and what record the routine produces.

03

Chain

Whether a product, transaction, inventory movement, or regulated event can be followed from one point to the next.

04

Authority

Who acted, whether that person or entity was qualified and permitted, and how the authorization is shown.

05

Exception

What happened when normal operations failed, changed, or required an unusual response—and what proof survived it.
