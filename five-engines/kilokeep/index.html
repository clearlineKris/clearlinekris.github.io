Engine 03 · Inventory

KiloKeep

Turn an inventory discrepancy into a question the team can resolve.

KiloKeep focuses on the difference between what the operation expects, what the records show, and what the team can actually verify.

What it adds to the stack An inventory-truth reasoning layer

RegMatrix supplies the regulatory context. KiloKeep helps organize the discrepancy, its surrounding events, and the record of resolution.

Operator benefit

Move from “it is off” to “here is why.”

01

Frame the discrepancy

Clarify what does not reconcile, which records are involved, and what must be verified before anyone adjusts the record.

02

Trace the context

Connect relevant movement, conversion, transfer, timing, and human handoffs without treating every mismatch as the same problem.

03

Document the resolution

Preserve what was reviewed, what was corrected, who authorized it, and what remains unresolved.

Stack fit

Add it when inventory questions keep becoming investigations.

KiloKeep belongs in a client-selected stack when discrepancies regularly cross teams, shifts, physical counts, source records, and inventory-tracking events.

Its benefit is disciplined reconciliation. It gives the team a common reasoning surface before an adjustment, explanation, or escalation is finalized.
