Compliance Operations Suite

Five Engines

The operational core of Veridion's product stack — five compliance engines built from years of on-the-ground cannabis operations.

What This Is

Each engine solves a specific compliance surface that operators face daily. They're not modules — they're independent systems that can run alone or together, each with its own data model, workflow logic, and operator interface. The silver backbone represents the shared infrastructure; the accent color is the engine's domain.

Shared Infrastructure

METRC integration, vault grounding, audit trails, and field tooling — built once, leveraged five times.

Domain-Specific Logic

Each engine encodes the regulatory nuance of its domain — inventory variance thresholds, COA validation rules, recall classification, waste witness protocols, vendor due diligence.

Operator-First Design

No admin dashboards. Every screen is built for the person doing the work — budtenders, compliance leads, warehouse managers, lab techs, procurement.

Evaluation Gates

GuaranGreen remains in evaluation. We don't ship vendor networks until the trust model is proven. The other four are active.
