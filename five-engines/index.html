Five Engines

The operational systems at the heart of ClearLine — five focused applications for the compliance work that has to hold up in the real world.

● Active · Inventory

KiloKeep

Inventory hygiene at scale. Keeps METRC records clean, reconciled, and audit-ready before discrepancies become larger problems.

● Active · Product integrity

PRFCTpack

Packaging, labeling, and product-record review designed to catch mismatches before inventory reaches the shelf.

● Active · Recall readiness

RecallRepel

Recall preparation and incident containment built to help operators identify affected product, preserve proof, and move with control.

● Active · Waste

RegulaGreen

Executable waste-compliance guidance that carries an operator from the applicable rule through destruction, documentation, and final record.

◌ In evaluation · Vendor confidence

GuaranGreen

A relationship and vendor-confidence system intended to support better due diligence across the cannabis supply chain.

The operating idea

One backbone. Five applications.

Each Engine addresses a different operational surface. They can stand alone or work together, while sharing ClearLine’s commitment to useful guidance, defensible records, and operator relief.

Shared infrastructure

Evidence capture, audit trails, and field-ready workflows provide a common operating foundation.

Domain-specific logic

Each Engine is shaped around the real rule language, records, and decisions of its own compliance surface.

Operator-first design

The work begins with the person who must perform, document, review, or defend the process.

Evaluation gates

An Engine does not move forward simply because the idea is attractive. Its trust model must hold first.
