Compliance Reasoning Architecture

Five Engines

Five connected reasoning systems at the heart of ClearLine—each focused on a different operational surface, all grounded by the same regulatory intelligence.

● Front-facing workflow · Waste

RegulaGreen

Executable waste-compliance guidance that carries an operator from the applicable rule through destruction, documentation, and the final record.

● Review system · Packaging

prfctPACK

Packaging, labeling, and product-record reasoning designed to catch mismatches before inventory reaches the shelf.

● Reasoning system · Inventory

KiloKeep

Inventory-truth logic for identifying discrepancies, understanding their context, and producing a record that can withstand review.

● Reasoning system · Recall readiness

RecallRepel

Recall preparation and incident-containment logic that helps identify affected product, preserve proof, and move with control.

◌ In evaluation · Relationship due diligence

GuaranGreen

A relationship and vendor-confidence system intended to support better due diligence across the cannabis supply chain.

The distinction

Not five disconnected apps.

The Engines are regulatory reasoning systems. Some become a tool an operator uses directly; others work behind ClearLine’s analysis and produce the practical guidance, review, or documentation an operator receives.

Direct workflow

RegulaGreen

The clearest front-facing Engine: live operational guidance from rule to documented waste record.

Focused review

prfctPACK

A review system that can meet the client directly when packaging and product records need examination.

Reasoning layers

KiloKeep · RecallRepel · GuaranGreen

Internal logic that strengthens the analysis and deliverables built around inventory, incidents, and relationships.

RegMatrix integration

Context enters. Useful work leaves.

RegMatrix supplies the state-specific law, enforcement posture, policy context, and sourced intelligence. Each Engine applies that context to one operating surface, then helps ClearLine produce something a person can use.

Engine philosophy

What every Engine must hold.

RegMatrix-grounded

The reasoning begins with sourced authority and the context around how a rule operates.

Operator-first

The work begins with the person who must perform, document, review, or defend the process.

Proof is the output

Documentation is not an afterthought. The record should show what happened and why.

Interoperable, not monolithic

Each Engine can reason independently while becoming stronger beside the others.

Human in the loop

Systems can assist the decision. Responsibility, judgment, and critical authorization remain human.

Trust before expansion

An Engine does not move forward simply because its concept is attractive. Its trust model must hold first.
