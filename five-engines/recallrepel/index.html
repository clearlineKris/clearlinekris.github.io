Prepare the response before urgency starts making decisions.

RecallRepel focuses the operation on affected product, responsible action, supporting proof, and the sequence needed to contain an incident with control.

What it adds to the stack A recall-readiness and containment layer

RegMatrix supplies the regulatory context. RecallRepel helps the team apply it to preparation, scope, action, and the incident record.

Operator benefit

Replace the scramble with a controlled response.

Define the scope

Help the team identify which product, records, locations, and movements require attention without widening the incident by assumption.
