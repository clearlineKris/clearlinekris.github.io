prfctPACK

Catch the mismatch before the package reaches the shelf.

prfctPACK focuses review on the relationship between the product, its package, its label, and the record meant to support it.

What it adds to the stack A focused pre-shelf review layer

RegMatrix supplies the applicable context. prfctPACK applies that context to the package and its supporting record.

Stack fit

Add it when packaging decisions cross several hands.

prfctPACK belongs in a client-selected stack when products, labels, vendors, records, and release decisions must stay aligned across multiple people or teams.

Its benefit is earlier clarity. The Engine gives packaging questions a dedicated review surface instead of leaving them scattered across inboxes, proofs, and product records.
